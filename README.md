### Hi, I'm Steven Byrne

Bioprocess scientist and data practitioner based in Melbourne, Australia. I spent 20 years in biopharmaceutical R&D and manufacturing, mostly in upstream cell culture process development for recombinant proteins, and I build data tools that turn lab instrument output into analysis-ready data.

I hold a Graduate Certificate in Data Science (RMIT University) and I'm currently completing a Master of Business Analytics and AI Strategy.

#### Projects

| Project | What it does | Built with |
|---|---|---|
| [ambr-data-pipeline](https://github.com/steven-byrne/ambr-data-pipeline) | Turns raw Ambr 250 micro-bioreactor files (online, feed, Nova metabolite and Vi-CELL data) into long and wide analysis-ready tables | Python, pandas, matplotlib, pytest |
| [vicell-processor](https://github.com/steven-byrne/vicell-processor) | Turns Vi-CELL cell counts into labelled seed train datasets with replicate statistics, doubling times and growth curves | Python, pandas, matplotlib, pytest |
| [SeedSync](https://github.com/steven-byrne/SeedSync) | Interactive seed train planner: compares expansion strategies and back-schedules passages from a bioreactor start date | R, Shiny, Plotly |

All three are rebuilt versions of tools I first wrote for my own lab work. They use generic or simulated data, so anyone can download and run them.
